## What this is

An Isabelle/HOL formalization using a project-adopted 262-character received
witness of the Xuanzang-attributed *Prajnaparamitahrdaya*, not the exact
260-character Taishō T251 body, parametric over
three semantics: classical two-valued logic, Belnap–Dunn First Degree
Entailment (FDE, four values), and Priest's FDE extended with a fifth,
"ineffable" value. Session `Buddhist_Comparative_Logic` lives under `isabelle/`; see
[`README.md`](../README.md) for how to install Isabelle and build it.

The public artifact is the research-software and formalization-corpus
repository `buddhist-comparative-logic`, displayed as **Buddhist and
Comparative Asian Logic Formalizations**. The Heart Sutra is one fully traced
case study. The source repository is public at
[takuya50/buddhist-comparative-logic](https://github.com/takuya50/buddhist-comparative-logic)
with package version `0.1.0`; its first GitHub release and tag, `v0.1.0`, remain
in preparation for the chosen release day, 2026-09-20. The software DOI
`10.5281/zenodo.22851717` is reserved, but the Zenodo draft deposit is not
published. No Software Heritage save has been submitted or SWHID recorded.
The paper is separate, unpublished, and intended only for figshare under its
own licence decision.

## What this is not

This is **not** a proof that the Heart Sutra's claims are true, that
Madhyamaka metaphysics is consistent, or that any particular reading of
"form is emptiness" is the correct one. Every theorem here is conditional
on the stated locale assumptions, which are themselves formal
reconstructions of the sutra's clauses — not independently established
facts. The path layer (`HeartSutra_Path.thy`) in particular proves only that the
sutra's own stated implications compose into its own stated conclusion.

## Evidence labels

The labels below are defined entirely within this standalone project.  They
describe what a proof assistant checked; they do not certify the truth of a
historical interpretation or of a theorem's premises.

| Label | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| `isabelle-theorem` | Isabelle accepted a `sorry`-free proof of the stated formula from the stated locale assumptions, under the pinned Isabelle version below. |
| `isabelle-theorem (definitional)` | As above, but the locale's assumptions are themselves a direct restatement of the sutra's clauses (the path layer): the theorem shows the stated implications compose, not that the implications hold. |
| `isabelle-countermodel` | A concrete valuation witnesses that some entailment does **not** hold (a constructive countermodel, e.g. `FiniteLogics.glut_p`), cross-checked where Nitpick's fragment allows by a `nitpick [expect = genuine]` regression. |
| `isabelle-executable` | A finite computation checked by Isabelle's code generator (`by eval` / `value`) over an enumeration proved exhaustive; the repository's `executable-certificate`, restricted to this session. |
| `isabelle-definition` | A datatype, function, or locale fixing terminology; no theorem is claimed about it beyond the stated cardinality/shape facts. |
| `noted` | The clause is not a candidate for propositional formalization (an imperative, a performative utterance, or a proper name); the reason is recorded next to an explicit non-formalizability lemma where one exists. |
| `open` | Not yet formalized. |

## Wording rules

- Never write "the sutra is true" or "the sutra is consistent". Always say
  "under reading `Rk` and logic `L`, clause `HSnn`'s formal counterpart is
  designated/derivable".
- "Proved" is used only together with a theorem name.
- A countermodel is described as "logic `L`'s reading does not validate
  ...", never as "logic `L` is wrong" or "logic `L` refutes the sutra".
- Every path-layer claim carries "(definitional)" and the sentence: "the
  assumptions are the sutra's own claims; this is not evidence for the
  doctrine."
- A clause whose formal reading differs across logics (see the
  logic-dependence column) must have both readings stated; STATUS never
  reports only the reading that happens to be more quotable.

## Base text

The project-adopted Japanese-style received witness: 262 characters in
the body (HS01–HS29), title (HS00) and colophon (HS30) outside the count.
The transmitted form includes 一切 in 遠離一切顛倒夢想 (HS22); the Taishō
text of T251 omits those two characters, giving a 260-character body.
The local witness is not transcribed from a named Japanese edition. Additional
mantra spellings differ without changing length. `SOURCES.md` and the
executable comparison report distinguish CBETA's running text from its Taishō
apparatus. These variants are documented, not additional formalization targets.

## Logic-dependent clauses

`HeartSutra_Coverage.logic_dependent_set = {HS06, HS07, HS09, HS10}` is the
machine-checked set of clauses whose formal reading is not the same across
the three logics. Of these, HS07 and HS09 are stated in a form
(`R_mutual`, `empty_of`) that happens to hold uniformly; HS06 and HS10 are
where classical, FDE, and FDE-plus-fifth-value genuinely diverge — see the
Note column above.

## The fifth value and the four corners

`Catuskoti.thy` shows that Priest's fifth value `e` is not a fifth
*koti* (corner): under the primary reading (`e` undesignated) it collapses
into corner K4 ("neither"), the same corner `N` already occupies; under the
compared variant (`e` designated) it collapses into K3 ("both"). This
directory does not claim "the catuṣkoṭi has five corners" — see
`Catuskoti.fifth_corner_value_level`, `fde5_koti_of_E`,
`fde5D_koti_of_E`.

## Cotnoir's objection, reproduced

`FiniteLogics.fde_mp_fails` and `fde5_mp_fails` give the constructive
countermodel (a glut, value `B`, on the antecedent: `FiniteLogics.glut_p`)
showing that FDE's material conditional does not validate modus ponens —
A. J. Cotnoir's objection to using FDE for Nāgārjuna's logic.
`FiniteLogics.fde_mp_bivalent` shows the failure needs the glut: on bivalent
valuations modus ponens is recovered. A Nitpick regression
(`nitpick [expect = genuine]`, on the explicitly quantified form of the
statement — the set-based `entails` form lies outside Nitpick's supported
fragment) is part of the build for both `fde` and `fde5`, so a change to
the truth tables that made the countermodel disappear would fail the build.

## Lean–Isabelle statement-level correspondence

The Lean 4 development has 115 files: 112 subject and infrastructure
modules plus the three aggregates `BuddhistComparativeLogic/Release.lean`,
`BuddhistComparativeLogic/Experimental.lean`, and `BuddhistComparativeLogic.lean`.  The 112 substantive
modules comprise 34 Isabelle-aligned modules and 78 Lean-only extensions;
`BuddhistComparativeLogic/Core/Foundation.lean` represents both `FiniteLogics` and `Catuskoti`.
The release aggregate contains 110 of those modules, while the experimental
aggregate contains two, retained pending exact source-to-formal mapping.  [`PORTING.md`](PORTING.md)
gives the complete Isabelle map. The aligned modules include
the text and clause coverage, generic and concrete semantics, philosophical
structures, explicit models and countermodels, executable finite checks, and
the general signed-literal decision procedure for FDE/FDE5.

The Lean/static stage of the 2026-09-20 full verification checked all 115
files against bare Lean 4.32.0 without Mathlib.  The build
rejects proof admissions, project axioms/constants, opaque or unsafe
declarations, partial definitions, and native-code proof shortcuts; treats
warnings as errors; derives the local import order; and builds in a temporary
directory.  Run it with `sh tools/check-lean.sh`.

The two developments are aligned semantically rather than by a
declaration-for-declaration translation.
Lean records replace Isabelle locales, some helper lemmas are absorbed into
larger proofs, and explicit valuations replace Nitpick regressions.  The
Isabelle theories remain the source for their full commentary and detailed
proof decomposition.

The checked alignment establishes the following repository-specific facts:

| Question | Answer |
| --- | --- |
| Are the finite-matrix results harder in Lean? | Each becomes a `decide` proof after replacing the valuation quantifier by a finite complete list, the same move `FiniteDecisionProcedures` makes with `by eval`. |
| How are Isabelle locales represented? | Lean uses explicit records. Assumptions therefore remain visible in theorem arguments, and concrete record values witness non-vacuity. |
| How are countermodels represented? | Named finite valuations replace Nitpick searches. Lean checks the resulting equalities and failed entailments in the kernel. |
| What does the general FDE proof add? | `BuddhistComparativeLogic/Core/FDEProof.lean` proves variable sharing, characterizes FDE5 as FDE plus atom containment, and verifies executable decision procedures over arbitrary decidable atom types. |

The two developments provide independent encodings of the headline
claims.  Isabelle retains concise locale composition and automatic model
search; Lean makes the records, executable witnesses, and dependency graph
fully explicit.
